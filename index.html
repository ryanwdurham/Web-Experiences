About

I came to web design through a background in QA and software test engineering, where I learned that great user experiences live in the details — balancing thoughtful visual design with usability, accessibility, responsiveness, and performance to create smoother, more intuitive digital experiences.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, I design modern websites for local businesses, creative projects, and personal brands. When I'm not building, you'll find me hiking, playing guitar, exploring epic fantasy worlds, or refining the subtle UI details most people never notice.
