About

QA Engineer turned
creative web builder.

"I combine 15+ years of testing experience with modern web design to build websites that look great — and actually work."

I came to web design through a background in QA and software test engineering, where I learned that great user experiences are built in the details, balancing thoughtful visual design with usability, accessibility, responsiveness, and performance to create smoother, more intuitive digital experiences.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, I design modern websites for local businesses, creative projects, and personal brands. When I’m not building online experiences, you’ll usually find me hiking, playing my guitar, getting lost in epic fantasy worlds, or refining the subtle UI details most people overlook.
